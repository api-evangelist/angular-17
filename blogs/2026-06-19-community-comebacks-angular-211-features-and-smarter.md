This community roundup features podcast episodes on Angular 20 engineering decisions and navigating tech-layoff career setbacks, plus Alain Chautard's recap of Angular 21.1 minor features and performance improvements. Fanis Prodromou demonstrates combining asynchronous validators with debouncing in Signal Forms to optimize API requests, and Martine Dowden offers guidance on migrating legacy structural directives to Angular's modern Control Flow syntax. French-language content covers dynamic field lists with Signal Forms and an interview with Matthieu Riegler on joining the Angular core team.
